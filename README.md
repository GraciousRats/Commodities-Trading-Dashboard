# Commodities-Trading-Dashboard
This interactive dashboard showing how data in the commodities' trading sector is used to make commercial decisions and which deals to approve in the current market. It covers copper, iron ore, thermal coal and platinum. The total book value is marked at R126.2m.
Rand values: the book is worth R126.2m, monthly gross margin is about R80m, copper is R196,710/t, and deal sizes run from R81m to R244m.

## The business behind the dashboard

The desk is the commercial arm of a South African mining and trading group.

1. Buys from producers in the Northern Cape (iron ore, copper), Mpumalanga (coal), Limpopo and North West (platinum, copper).
Sells to smelters, steel mills and power utilities in Asia, Europe and South Africa.

3. Ships by rail to Richards Bay, Saldanha Bay and Durban, then by sea under CIF (seller pays freight and insurance) or FOB (buyer takes over at the port) terms.

The dashboard brings all three together in one view, so a trader or manager can see the position and act on it.
