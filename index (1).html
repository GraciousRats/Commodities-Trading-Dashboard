Definitions and data notes
Mark-to-market (MTM)
Profit or loss on open positions if they were closed at today’s prices.
Value at Risk (VaR)
The loss the book should not exceed on 95% of trading days. Each commodity has its own VaR limit.
Hedge ratio
Hedged position divided by net physical position. The headline figure weights each commodity by its value.
Credit headroom
Credit limit minus what the counterparty already owes, minus the new deal’s notional. Each deal is scored on its own, as if the others were not done.
About the data
All prices, positions, counterparties and deals are invented for demonstration. Counterparty names are fictional. The raw data is in the data/ folder of this repository.
